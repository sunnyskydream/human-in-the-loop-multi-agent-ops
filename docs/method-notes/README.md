# Method notes

Measurement contracts for governed systems that select which records or decisions receive deeper effort.

These differ from the [operating notes](../operating-notes/): operating notes begin with a failure and explain how it was caught; method notes begin with a measurement question and define the denominator, evidence boundary, and condition that could change the decision.

Read them in order. The first covers intake; the second covers decision scoring.

| # | Note | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Measure Segment Density Before You Scale the Motion](./01-segment-density.md) | Whether a source contains enough target-class records to justify scaled qualification, and whether rejection and deduplication controls expose their coverage. |
| 02 | [A Score Is Not Evidence Until Its Outcomes Are Comparable](./02-scoring-validation.md) | Whether labels, denominators, outcome maturity, and model provenance support a valid comparison of scored cohorts. |

Both report measurements from one governed, human-in-the-loop workflow. They are measurement contracts and bounded case evidence, not benchmarks or claims that the observed rates transfer to another system.
